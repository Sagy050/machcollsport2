    Machabeng College, International School of Lesotho

    An international school in Maseru that aims to give excellent pre-university education and to be one of Lesotho’s leading places to learn. Sports Day is where that community gets to play together.

    Maseru, since 1977

    The school opened in 1977 under the rules of Lesotho’s Ministry of Education and serves local and international families.

    IGCSE and IB Diploma

    Students prepare for the International GCSE and the International Baccalaureate, and many go on to universities abroad.

    Setsoto Stadium

    Lesotho’s national stadium in central Maseru is the venue for Sports Day on 20 November 2026.

    A royal patron

    Her Majesty Queen ’Masenate Mohato Seeiso is the school’s Patron and a former Machabeng student.
