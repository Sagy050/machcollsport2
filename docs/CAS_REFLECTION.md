# CAS Reflection (to be completed by the team)

This is a template. The reflection must be written in your own words, from your own experience of the project; the prompts below follow the seven IB CAS learning outcomes. Aim for 2 to 4 honest sentences each, with a specific example.

| Learning outcome | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 1. Identify own strengths and develop areas for growth | Which parts of the project did you do well, and where did you need to learn something new? |
| 2. Demonstrate that challenges have been undertaken, developing new skills | What was hard (for example real-time updates, two people editing at once, security) and what did you learn from solving it? |
| 3. Demonstrate how to initiate and plan a CAS experience | How did you plan the work between now and 17 November, and what did you change along the way? (For example: the recording screen was redesigned after talking to the people who will use it, from typing names to tapping athletes.) |
| 4. Show commitment to and perseverance in CAS experiences | When did it get difficult or time was short, and how did you keep going? |
| 5. Demonstrate the skills and recognise the benefits of working collaboratively | How did you divide the work and resolve disagreements in your group? What did you learn from the competing group's approach? |
| 6. Demonstrate engagement with issues of global significance | Who benefits from fair, transparent, accessible results (students, officials, parents)? How did you think about accessibility, security and data privacy of students' names? |
| 7. Recognise and consider the ethics of choices and actions | How did you handle student data, test accounts and passwords responsibly? How did you ensure scoring was fair and correctable? |

Evidence to attach: screenshots of the app, test output (`docs/TESTING.md`), a team log or photos of planning sessions, and notes on the day itself.
